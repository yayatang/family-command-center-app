Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Family Command Center is a private, single-household application used only by its owner.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads events from, and adds events to, the owner's own Google Calendar so they can be shown on a display at home.

Where data goes

Calendar data is processed on the owner's own computer and shown on a screen on the home network. It is not stored on any external server, and it is not sold, shared with or transferred to anyone else. It is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google user data

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
